Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how DevToolsHub handles information when visitors access and use our website.

2. Information processed by our tools

JSON Converter: JSON content is processed locally in your browser.

Image Compressor: Selected images are processed using the browser Canvas API.

Glassmorphism Generator: Design settings are processed locally to generate CSS.

QR Generator: QR codes are generated using a third-party JavaScript library loaded through a CDN.

These tools do not intentionally transmit the entered tool content or selected image files to a DevToolsHub server.
