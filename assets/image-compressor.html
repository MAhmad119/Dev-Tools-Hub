How to compress images online

  1. Select an image from your device.
  2. Enter your preferred maximum width.
  3. Choose the output format.
  4. Adjust image quality.
  5. Compress and download your image.

How does this image compressor work?

The tool uses the browser's HTML Canvas API to resize and export images. No dedicated image-processing backend is required.

Image quality and formats

JPEG and WebP support adjustable lossy quality. PNG export uses lossless encoding and generally ignores the quality setting.

Privacy

Selected images are processed locally in the browser and are not intentionally uploaded to our website.
