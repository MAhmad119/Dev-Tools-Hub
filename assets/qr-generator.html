How to create a QR code

  1. Enter your text or URL.
  2. Choose an optional center logo.
  3. Select the desired QR code dimensions.
  4. Click Generate QR Code.
  5. Download the PNG image.

What is a QR code?

A QR code is a two-dimensional barcode that can store text, website URLs and other information. Compatible camera applications can scan it.

Tips for better QR codes

Use high-contrast colors, keep the center logo small and leave enough white space around the code. Always test the final image with a scanning device.

Third-party library

QR generation uses QRCode.js, loaded from an external CDN. An internet connection is required to load this library.
